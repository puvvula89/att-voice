# Cricket IVR Agentic AI Opportunities

The following use cases represent opportunities associated with leveraging an AI agent to address Cricket wireless customers' needs when calling into Care (611 / 800-CRICKET) for support. They are listed in priority order based on their business value.

## AI Agents

### 1. Payment Resolution

Offer upfront the option of a billing agent to assist with payments and all questions regarding the customer's bill.

- Take payment
- Offer BridgePay and enroll
- Explain why this month's bill is different from previous
- If warranted, offer a nominal credit (i.e., $5) to address concern
- Offer AutoPay and enroll
- Update a card on file (if that is the reason for failure, or if the card will expire within 1 month)

### 2. Troubleshooting

Identify the customer's technical support intent, validate this back with the customer, and use conversational AI to guide the customer through appropriate steps to resolve their issue.

- Issue may be related to connectivity or device
- Data sources may include, but are not limited to, the Cricket website, device tutorials, and/or troubleshooting tools

### 3. Add Feature

Understand customer intent, know what features the customer currently has and what features they are eligible for. Based on that, explain and offer the feature — for example:

- Cricket Protect (insurance)
- International Long Distance
- Cricket Passport (roaming)

### 4. Plan Change

Perform a rate plan change to a higher tier plan given customer need. For example, the customer currently does not have mobile hotspot, or calls to add more data.

> Do not recommend lower tier plans, as those would not meet the needs of this use case.

### 5. Store Finder

Assist customers in finding the closest Cricket Wireless store, given:

- current location,
- billing address, or
- customer-stated location.

Then offer store hours of operation and directions. Finally, ask if the customer is looking to purchase a device and let the customer know if it is available in that store's inventory.
