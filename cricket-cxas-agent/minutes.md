**Subject:** Thank you, and next steps on the Cricket troubleshooting agent

Thank you for your time this afternoon. It was a pleasure meeting everyone, and we appreciated the open discussion and the questions that took us well beyond the demo itself.

**What we showed**

A single CX Agent Studio (CXAS) agent that handles both voice and chat, built on our latest `gemini-composite-v2` model and grounded in the troubleshooting tutorials from your own support site. The architecture is attached for reference.

**What we discussed**

- **SMS channel.** The same agent can serve your SMS channel. A thin middle layer bridges the customer's messages and the CXAS agent, so there is one agent to build and maintain across voice, chat and SMS.
- **A focused starting point.** We can pilot the troubleshooting agent on CXAS first. When a customer asks about something outside troubleshooting, the agent hands control back to your IVR, which routes the customer as it does today.
- **The north star.** Over time, CXAS becomes your care concierge, the approach AT&T is implementing today. It identifies customer intent at the front door, and you bring use cases under one umbrella at your own pace.
- **Beyond the virtual agent.** CXAS is one part of Gemini Enterprise for CX. Agent Assist, Insights and Quality AI work alongside it to support your customers and your live agents end to end.
